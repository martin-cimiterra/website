About Me

Welcome to my academic website, where I share information about my research, publications, and teaching activities.

I am an Assistant Professor of Economics (Maître de conférences) at Université Paris-Saclay, where I am affiliated with the Research Center in Economics and Management (RITM). I teach economics at the IUT de Sceaux.

Before joining Université Paris-Saclay in 2025, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Trento, Italy, where I worked on regional innovation and the green and digital transitions.

My research interests lie at the intersection of economics of innovation, economic geography, industrial dynamics, and the digital economy. I am particularly interested in technological change, regional innovation, and the interactions between green and digital transitions.

My research relies on applied econometrics, quantitative methods, and large-scale data analysis, using Python, R, Stata, and SQL.
